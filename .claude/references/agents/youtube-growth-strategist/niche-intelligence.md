# YouTube Niche Intelligence Reference

**Source:** "I Ranked Every YouTube Niche and It Got Ugly" by Adam Erhart (2026)
**Creator credentials (Adam Erhart's, as stated in the video):** 14 years on YouTube, launched his current channel from zero to 33K+ subs / 1M+ views in <1 year with 36 long-form videos (no Shorts, no paid ads)

---

## The 5 Niche Scoring Factors

Every niche should be evaluated on these 5 measurable dimensions:

| # | Factor | What It Measures |
|---|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Money** | Revenue potential BEYOND AdSense — sponsorships, courses, coaching, digital products, merch, communities, affiliate deals. AdSense is the smallest piece for most full-time creators. |
| 2 | **Competition** | How saturated is the niche? More supply = harder to break through. |
| 3 | **Niche Growth** | Is the space expanding or slowly dying? |
| 4 | **Staying Power** | Can you make 200+ videos without hitting a content wall? |
| 5 | **Beginner Friendliness** | How easy is it for a new creator to break into the space? |

**Key insight:** A finance creator with 50K subs selling a $500 coaching package makes MORE money than a gaming channel with 1M subs on AdSense only.

## Niche Tier Rankings (2026)

### S Tier — Launch Pad (Giant Opportunity)

| Niche | Why S-Tier | Revenue Model | Notes |
|-------|-----------|---------------|-------|
| **Finance & Business** | Highest CPMs on YouTube. Viewers need help (not entertainment), so they buy. Banks, software companies, investment apps pay premium. | Courses, coaching, consulting, products, communities, affiliates | Must KNOW your stuff — AI slop flooding the space and viewers can tell. "If you've done the thing you're teaching, that's your unfair advantage." |
| **Service Providers** (dentists, realtors, lawyers, plumbers) | YouTube as marketing vehicle, not creator career. One client from a video = thousands in lifetime value. | Client acquisition (the business IS the revenue) | Low competition — most professionals think YouTube is "for influencers." A chiropractor doesn't need 1M subs, just a few thousand local viewers. |
| **Tech & AI Tutorials** | Everyone needs to learn new AI tools. Ads pay well. | Courses, communities, templates, consulting, tool affiliate programs | Getting saturated FAST. Basic screen recordings won't cut it anymore. Must go deep on specific software for specific audience. "AI tools for small business owners" > "tech tutorials." |

### A Tier — Super Strong

| Niche | Why A-Tier | Revenue Model | Notes |
|-------|-----------|---------------|-------|
| **Storytelling** (Reddit stories, true crime, history, mythology) | "Probably the most slept-on niche on YouTube right now." Big watch time, tons of topics, way less crowded. | Podcast spin-offs, Patreon, early access communities, potential book deals | Don't need face on camera. AI slop flooding in = opportunity for quality. "Demand for good storytelling at all-time high, supply of quality at all-time low." Need good voice + tight unique angle. |
| **Automotive & Outdoor** (car builds, hunting, fishing, camping, overlanding) | Obsessed audiences who watch everything, join communities, buy gear. | Affiliate links (fishing rods, camping gear, auto parts, tools, apparel) | Hunting channel with 40K subs + smart affiliate links can outearn general entertainment channel with 500K subs. Competition exists but not a bloodbath. Never run out of content. |
| **Fitness, Cooking, DIY** | Emotional connection — viewers see creators they want to be like. Builds loyalty and repeat viewership. | Programs, coaching, meal plans, supplements, apparel, cookbooks, templates, courses | Going too broad is brutal. "Fitness" = war zone. "Fitness for busy dads over 40" = ownable lane. Specificity wins. |
| **Real Estate & Location** | Millions research where to move every year via YouTube. | Mortgage/moving service CPMs + direct lead generation for realtors | Content stays relevant for years. "Top 10 places to live in [city]" = evergreen lead machine. |

### B Tier — Doable, But Harder

| Niche | Why B-Tier | Key Challenge |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| **Vlogging** | Everyone can film their day — that's the problem. Gets almost no search traffic. | Must be a storyteller, not a documenter. "Boring vlogs are dead. A good vlog has a story." Study Casey Neistat and Emma Chamberlain. |
| **Product Reviews** | Niche reviews (camping gear, home office, budget tools) are solid. General tech reviews = fighting the big boys. | Affiliate money is good but honesty is essential. "Reviewers who tell you when something sucks are the ones that build trust." |
| **Sports Commentary** | Loyal audiences, sports betting affiliates pay well. | Time-sensitive content treadmill. A playoff breakdown gets views for a week; a business video gets views for years. |

### C Tier — Really Hard

| Niche | Why C-Tier | Key Challenge |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| **Gaming** | Massive demand BUT massive supply. Most content looks identical. | Must have personality that carries content (mute gameplay and still entertaining), consistency, and play relevant games. Treat it like a business, not a hobby. |
| **Trend/Reaction/Celebrity News** | Content doesn't last. Short shelf life (days). | Content hamster wheel. No off-platform revenue. "Nobody's buying much from reaction channels." Must build brand around unique take. |

### D Tier — TRAP (Avoid)

| Niche | Why D-Tier | The Trap |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **Faceless AI Channels / Clip Farms / Compilations** | Beginner score: 8/10 (highest). Staying power: 2/10 (lowest). Easy to start, nearly impossible to keep alive. | YouTube is ACTIVELY DELETING these channels (not just demonetizing — gone overnight, no warning, no appeal). Gurus selling courses caught a wave years ago that's already gone, showing screenshots from 3 years ago. Nobody buys from faceless AI channels — no merch, no community, no coaching. Only revenue is ads, which can vanish overnight. |
| **Fake "Make Money Online"** | Massive trust problem. Recycled advice, unverifiable income screenshots. | YouTube striking down channels with huge income claims. "More Photoshop experience than actual business acumen." Legit business education = S tier. Get-rich-quick = D tier. |

## Critical Distinctions for Your Channels

### A quality faceless channel ≠ Faceless AI Slop

The D-tier "faceless AI" warning targets:
- Channels that scrape Reddit posts with robot voices and generic B-roll
- Clip farms repackaging others' content with no original thought
- AI-generated visual-only channels with zero personality or expertise

**A quality faceless channel is DIFFERENT because:**
1. **It sits at a strong niche intersection** — e.g. an S-tier topic (business, finance, education) delivered through A-tier storytelling
2. **Original analysis and frameworks** — not scraped/recycled content
3. **A specific, ownable angle** — a narrow lane with little direct competition
4. **Educational intent** — viewers are there to learn, not passively consume
5. **Off-platform revenue potential** — courses, books, consulting, coaching built on the channel's expertise

**However, the warning is still relevant:**
- The production pipeline must produce quality that is clearly NOT AI slop
- Voice quality matters enormously — your TTS engine must sound authoritative, not robotic
- Visual quality must exceed generic stock footage wallpaper
- Scripts must demonstrate genuine expertise and original thinking
- Each video needs a unique angle that couldn't be generated by prompting ChatGPT

### Music compilation channels

Music compilation channels fall in the warning zone. Differentiation strategy:
- Curated taste > algorithmic selection
- Community building around the genre's culture
- Consistent brand identity and audio quality
- Artist discovery angle (not just playlists)

### Finance and business channels

S-tier niche by default. Key success factor: "Must KNOW your stuff — AI slop flooding the space."

## Universal Principles (Apply to ALL Channels)

1. **"Your niche gets you found, but YOU are what makes people stay."** — Personality and expertise retention, not just topic selection
2. **Specificity wins** — "Fitness for busy dads over 40" beats "Fitness." The more specific, the less competition and more audience trust
3. **Most quit before 20 long-form videos** — Persistence IS the strategy
4. **AdSense is the smallest revenue piece** — Build for courses, coaching, products, communities, affiliates
5. **YouTube rewards those who keep uploading** — Consistency + focus + persistence = the whole game
6. **Don't go broad** — Pick a lane, own it. "Everything to everybody = nothing to anyone"
7. **AI flooding = opportunity for quality** — When everyone's producing AI slop, real quality stands out more than ever
8. **Show the work, not the screenshot** — Authenticity and demonstrated expertise build trust

## Niche Evaluation Checklist (Use Before Launching Any New Channel)

Before greenlighting a new channel, score it:

```
[ ] Money: Can this audience buy products/services? (Score 1-10)
[ ] Competition: How saturated? (Score 1-10, 10 = least saturated)
[ ] Niche Growth: Expanding or dying? (Score 1-10)
[ ] Staying Power: Can we make 200+ videos? (Score 1-10)
[ ] Beginner Friendly: Can we break in? (Score 1-10)
[ ] Off-Platform Revenue: What can we sell beyond ads?
[ ] Differentiation: What's our unique angle that AI can't replicate?
[ ] Quality Bar: Can we consistently produce above AI-slop level?

Total: ___/50 (Target: 35+ for green light)
```
